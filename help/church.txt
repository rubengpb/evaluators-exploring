The REPL works with pure lambda-calculus. There are not constant, prebuilt
functions... This syntax sugar transforms lists and numbers into the
tradicional Church encoding.

When you write, "1 2" it is traduced to "(\s.\z.s z) (\s.\z.s (s z))" and
"[1;a;[(\x.x); f]]" to "\c.\n.c (\s.\z.s z) (c a (c (\c.\n.c (\x.x) (c f n)) n))"

You can algo display the result of evaluation.

    Usefull commands:
      :set church on          -> enable list and number display as Church encoding
      :set church list on     -> enable list display as Church encoding
      :set church number off  -> disable number display as Church encoding
